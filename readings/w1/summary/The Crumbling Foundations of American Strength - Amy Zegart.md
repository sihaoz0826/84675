# The Crumbling Foundations of American Strength — Fast Read

**Amy Zegart**, *Foreign Affairs*, Sept/Oct 2024. Subtitle: *Knowledge Is Power—and the United States Is Losing It*.

**What this is:** A policy essay on why U.S. power now comes from knowledge and new tech, not tanks and land.

**Thesis:** Future U.S. strength depends less on blocking China from American tech and more on building education, research, and breakthroughs at home.

- Ukraine held off Russia not because it spent more on defense (Russia ranked 5th; Ukraine 36th) but because educated people built drones and homemade weapons. Old power metrics missed this.
- For centuries, power was stuff governments could count and control: armies, land, navies, oil. Today it is knowledge and tech (like AI) that spread easily and cannot be handed back once they are out.
- Private companies now shape world politics — Meta for billions of users, Musk for Ukraine’s Starlink — and their interests are not always America’s.
- Washington’s own tools are breaking: key jobs sit empty, the U.S. now spends more on debt interest than on defense, and Pentagon stopgap budgets fund old weapons instead of new ideas. Schools and research universities are sliding too.
- “Knowledge power” means two things: **innovate** (make and use breakthroughs) and **anticipate** (classic spy work plus understanding tech built at home). It looks inward: help America thrive no matter what China does.
- You measure it by how well kids learn, whether top talent clusters in labs, and whether universities can still do basic research (slow, open-ended science that later gave us radar, GPS, the Internet, mRNA vaccines, and AI).
- U.S. K–12 is in trouble: 13-year-olds’ math and reading are the worst in decades; 15-year-olds rank 34th in the world in math.
- The U.S. leans on foreign talent, then often makes them leave. Computer-science and electrical-engineering doctorates going to U.S. citizens fell from 78% (1980) to 32% (2022). Immigrants built nearly half of Fortune 500 firms, but the visa system wastes that advantage.
- China now publishes more top-cited papers. Federal research funding fell from 1.9% of GDP (1964) to 0.7% (2020). More than 70% of new U.S. AI Ph.D.s go straight to industry, draining the professors who teach the next generation.
- The fix: compare U.S. and foreign tech the way the Pentagon compares armies; build a national “compute reserve” (cheap advanced chips for researchers, not just big tech); let more top students stay; fix Pentagon buying; restore basic-research money; treat K–12 as national security and pay the best teachers more.

## Lines worth remembering

- Knowledge is the ultimate portable weapon.
- Knowledge power looks inward: innovate and anticipate.
- Educate the world’s best students, then make them leave — a self-sabotaging talent system.
- Talent follows talent.
- Blocking China will not invent America’s future.
- Knowledge is power — and it must be cultivated at home.
