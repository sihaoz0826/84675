# Xi Jinping — Strive to Become the World's Primary Center for Science and High Ground for Innovation

**What this is:** A May 2018 speech by Xi Jinping to China’s science and engineering academies. DigiChina (Stanford) translated it. *Qiushi* published it in March 2021 as China released the 14th Five-Year Plan — a signal that the Plan continues work already underway.

**Thesis:** If China is to be strong and “rejuvenated,” it must become the world’s main science center and innovation high ground — a world science-and-tech superpower (世界科技强国).

- **A new tech wave is remaking the world.** AI, quantum, telecom, biology, robots, new energy, space, and the oceans are breaking through at once. Fields are fusing. Science now shapes a nation’s fate and people’s lives more than ever.

- **China is still behind.** Basic research is weak. Industry ignores it. China lacks big original breakthroughs. Key tools — machine tools, high-end chips, basic software, algorithms, parts, materials — are still bottlenecks. **Core technologies are still controlled by others.** Research does not turn into products well. Talent, pay, and government rules do not fit a superpower.

- **This is a crossroads.** The world tech revolution meets China’s shift in how it grows. Some crossroads come again; some you miss. The moment, the challenge, and the mission are all pressing. Scientists must aim at the world frontier and be **pacesetters**.

- **Innovation is the first driving force.** Use science to raise the quality of the economy, not just its size. Fuse the internet, big data, and AI with factories and farms. Make **intelligent manufacturing** the main upgrade path. Push Chinese industry toward the high end of global value chains.

- **Own the core: independent innovation.** **Self-reliance** is how China stands in the world. **Independent (indigenous) innovation** (自主创新) is the only path up the tech peaks. Innovation fails most of the time — still persist. Key slogan: **core technologies cannot be asked for, bought, or begged.** Only owning them protects the economy and defense. Shift from **following → running alongside → leading**. Basic research is the source of the whole system: sit on the “cold bench,” plant trees, dig wells. Then open the “last kilometer” so samples become products, then goods for sale.

- **Reform the system.** Science is the field that most needs constant reform. Drive change with two wheels: **tech innovation + institutional reform**. Firms should be the main innovators; the market should decide where research money goes. Funding should serve people’s creative work, not the reverse. Fuse civilian and military tech. Protect intellectual property.

- **Stay open, but raise China’s voice.** Independent innovation is not closed-door work. Use the world’s labs, people, and money. Turn Belt and Road into an innovation road. Raise China’s **rule-making power** in global science governance.

- **Talent is the real foundation.** Hard power and soft power both rest on talent. Stop judging people by papers, titles, degrees, and “hats.” Judge ability, quality, and real contribution. Attract talent from everywhere and let them compete.

## Lines worth remembering

- Goal: world’s primary science center and innovation high ground; a world S&T superpower
- Core tech cannot be asked for, bought, or begged — China must own it
- Follow → run alongside → lead
- Innovation is the first driving force; firms, not just labs, should lead; stay open, not closed
- Talent over papers, titles, and hats; funding serves people, not the reverse
