## Required seven slides (exact wording)

> Create one slide for each of the following questions:

> (1) What is the product? How does it work? Discuss core system functions, critical components/subsystems, and architecture in which the components are embedded.

> (2) Lifecycle: Draw a flow diagram to depict the development and diffusion lifecycle. Key milestones: conceptualization → prototype → scaling → dominant design/platform.

> (3) Inspiration: How did the idea originate? Was it potentially inspired by a science fiction concept, military or market demand, both, or something else?

> (4) Design: Is the product you chose recombinant or radical? Why? Include a basic genealogy if preceded by an analogous technology and explain key differences in hardware and use-cases.

> (5) Cooperation: How are development and parts sourced? Which countries are involved at which points? Applicable export control regimes or standards?

> (6) Applications: Spin‑on / spin‑off / dual‑use / single‑use / general purpose? Show evidence for your classification and any trajectory shifts (e.g., civilian → military).

> (7) Implications: Is the product you chose an incremental or disruptive innovation? Did it create new markets? Displace dominant companies? Other effects?

(*Source: `sources/Syllabus_ 84-375 Geopolitics of Innovation (2026) (4).pdf`, p. 3.*)
